Make digital products that hold up.

The ByJTT Design Standard is a practical guide to product purpose, accessibility, interaction, content, AI-assisted work and production quality.

What this is

A public working standard for people making digital products with or without AI.

It helps teams make important decisions explicit and check whether the finished product lives up to them.

Principles

Start with what matters.

Good interfaces follow from a clear problem, sound evidence and realistic conditions.

01

Start with people

Understand users, goals, constraints, risks and what success looks like.

02

Use evidence

Base important decisions on user needs, product requirements, platform guidance or a clear design reason.

03

Design real conditions

Include varied content, devices, states, errors and different ways of interacting.

04

Make it accessible

Build semantics, focus, contrast, zoom, motion and assistive-technology support into the product.

05

Reuse good solutions

Use established standards and mature systems before creating something new.

06

Test the real product

A screenshot is not proof. Validate the implementation people will actually use.

AI + agents

Keep automation understandable.

People should know what an AI system suggested, created, checked and actually changed.

Suggested → Generated → Inspected → Verified → Executed → Human-approved

Use clear states for meaningful actions. The stronger the consequence, the clearer the evidence and user control should be.

Inclusive design

Make access part of the product.

Design for different abilities, devices, preferences and environments from the start.

Interaction

Provide usable paths for keyboard, touch, pointer and assistive technology where relevant.

Content

Expect long text, errors, missing media, localisation and machine-generated content.

Adaptation

Support useful preferences without hiding meaning or taking control away from the user.

Evidence

State what was tested and what remains unknown. Do not turn an automated score into a promise.
