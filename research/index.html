Research

Evidence for better design decisions.

ByJTT research looks at user needs, competitors, established patterns, accessibility, AI-design failure modes and emerging tools — separating what is observed from what is inferred or still a hypothesis.

Research rule

Important claims should be traceable to evidence. Marketing language is not a substitute for a finding.

AI design & user pain

Where generated interfaces become generic, confusing, inaccessible or disconnected from the underlying product problem.

Patterns & conventions

What mature products and platforms already solve well, and when reuse beats custom invention.

Accessibility

Keyboard interaction, focus, contrast, zoom, reduced motion and assistive technology are treated as product requirements.

Tools & ecosystem

Research remains provider-neutral so the product can adopt the best available execution tools without making them the architecture.

Research informs the workflow; it does not replace judgement.

Findings become requirements, design rationale, benchmark inputs or validation rules only when their implications are understood and recorded.
