Why it exists

AI can produce a polished first draft quickly. Good products still need clear purpose, sound decisions, accessible interactions, realistic content and evidence that the result works.

This system makes those decisions easier to explain, test and reuse.

A practical workflow

Start with the problem. Finish with evidence.

A simple path from an idea to something people can actually use.

01 · Understand

Define the problem

Clarify who the product helps, what they need and what success means.

02 · Research

Check what is known

Use user evidence, established patterns, platform guidance and relevant alternatives.

03 · Specify

Record the important decisions

Make requirements, constraints and open questions visible.

04 · Design

Make purposeful choices

Use the Standard and Contracts to turn intent into an interface.

05 · Validate

Test the result

Check usability, accessibility, content, responsive behaviour and implementation reality.

06 · Learn

Keep what works

Record useful evidence and improve the system over time.

Inclusive design

Build for different people and different ways of using a product.

Accessibility belongs in the design from the beginning.

Interaction

Support keyboard, touch, pointer and assistive technology where applicable.

Perception

Use readable text, sufficient contrast, meaningful structure and information that does not rely on colour alone.

Cognition

Keep language clear, flows predictable and recovery possible when something goes wrong.

Resilience

Design for long content, missing media, empty and error states, localisation and changing conditions.

A living standard

v0.1 will change as we learn.

New guidance should be backed by research, testing, production evidence or useful feedback. Changes stay versioned so people can see what changed and why.

Questions

Start where you are.

Who is this for?

Anyone involved in making a digital product, including founders, designers, developers and people working with AI tools.

Is this an AI website builder?

The Studio can help create a website, but the wider project is about making design decisions clear, testable and reusable.

Can AI systems use it?

Yes. The Standard and Contracts are available in machine-readable forms so AI systems can work from explicit requirements.

Does it guarantee good design?

No. It gives teams a stronger process for making and checking decisions. Real users and real evidence still matter.
