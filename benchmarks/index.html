Benchmarks

Compare workflows without pretending the numbers prove everything.

ByJTT benchmarks use controlled briefs, explicit constraints, reproducible conditions and documented scoring so differences can be inspected rather than turned into vague superiority claims.

Evidence standard

Results are labelled with their limitations. A benchmark can be directional evidence without being causal proof.

01 — Freeze

Same starting conditions

Briefs, constraints and relevant context are frozen before comparison.

02 — Run

Separate treatments

Candidate workflows are executed without contaminating the comparison.

03 — Evaluate

Independent checks

Objective gates and defined scoring are kept separate from the generator's own assessment.

04 — Report

Show limitations

Methods, evidence and uncertainty travel with the result.

05 — Learn

Improve the system

Accepted and rejected outcomes can inform future contracts, tooling and validation.

06 — Repeat

Keep it reproducible

Benchmark infrastructure is designed for controlled follow-up rather than one-off demos.
