The idea behind ByJTT

Good-looking is only the beginning.

AI can make a screen look finished before the hard decisions have been made. ByJTT helps people notice what is missing, make the reasoning visible, and keep the useful parts of human judgment in the loop.

“The goal isn’t to make more screens. It’s to make better decisions before the screen exists.”

When you have a first version

Check a design without needing design vocabulary.

Describe what you made or paste a local link. This quick reflection helps you find the gaps worth fixing first. It is a starting point, not a score.

Look for these three things first

  • Can someone tell what to do next without being taught?
  • Does the design still make sense when content is long, missing or slow?
  • Can a keyboard user reach the important actions and understand the result?
