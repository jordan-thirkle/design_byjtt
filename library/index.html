Resource anatomy

Every serious resource should be portable.

Intent

What the resource is trying to accomplish, who it serves and where it fits.

Design contract

Tokens, structure, components, interactions, responsive rules and accessibility requirements.

Decision trace

Why meaningful changes happened, including the instruction that caused them and what changed.

Evidence

Executable checks and their status, not a hand-written “looks good” badge.
