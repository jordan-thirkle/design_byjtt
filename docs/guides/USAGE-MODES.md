# Guided and professional usage

The two modes change presentation and control density, not truth or quality thresholds.

## Guided mode

Guided mode is for people who want the system to supply missing design expertise. It should translate domain checks into clear questions, identify blocking unknowns before generation, explain problems and evidence in plain language, distinguish apparent from verified completeness, and offer fix, show-evidence and teach-me paths without disparaging the user.

## Professional mode

Professional mode exposes contract versions, evidence strength, exceptions, provenance, raw validation, conformance results and explicit overrides with rationale. It supports deterministic repository/CI integration without forced tutorials.

Mode selection belongs in the Design Contract and compiled Design Context Package. An adapter may format information for a target, but it may not weaken hard constraints or silently resolve unknowns.
