# Plausibility debt, the Demo Trap and design slop

AI can remove the execution barrier without removing the expertise barrier. That is valuable: more people can build digital products. It also creates a new review problem because a plausible interface can hide decisions and states that were never resolved.

**Plausibility debt** is the accumulated risk created when output looks sufficiently correct that missing reasoning, context, validation or craft becomes difficult to notice. Like technical debt, it may remain invisible until change or real use applies pressure.

The **Demo Trap** is the narrower failure mode in which a workflow optimizes for an impressive happy-path demonstration: tidy content, ideal data, one viewport and no failure states. A demo is evidence that a demo works—not evidence that a product is ready.

For ByJTT, **design slop** is not a forbidden visual style. It is the condition where apparent completeness exceeds the reasoning, context, validation and craft supporting the result. A gradient, card or familiar pattern can be entirely appropriate when it serves intent. The same element is a warning signal when it appears only because it is a model default.

## The non-designer expertise gap

Vibe coders, founders, students, PMs and developers are not the problem. AI gives them legitimate new agency. The gap is that someone cannot reasonably request or review concepts they do not yet know exist: focus management, information architecture, dangerous-action recovery, localization expansion, content resilience or partial-data behavior.

ByJTT's response is to avoid requiring users to know what they do not know. Guided mode surfaces the missing questions and explains why they matter. Professional mode exposes the same evidence, decisions, contracts, exceptions and controls without mandatory teaching. Both modes use the same source of truth and Gauntlet.

The objective is not to eliminate designers. It is to raise the floor for beginners while raising the ceiling and reducing repetitive governance work for professionals.
