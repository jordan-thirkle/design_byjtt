# v0.1 foundation architecture

`design.byjtt.com` and this repository are the canonical source of truth. External design and generation tools are adapters and benchmark targets, never architectural dependencies.

## Mission

Make excellent design knowledge accessible to both humans and AI building digital products—raising the floor for vibe coders and non-designers while raising the ceiling for professionals.

## Evidence-to-validation chain

```text
pain signal → evidence → design decision → versioned contract
                                        ↓
                           Design Context Package
                                        ↓
                         target-specific adapter
                                        ↓
                         output → Gauntlet evidence
```

The v0.1 schemas live in [`../../schemas/v0.1/`](../../schemas/v0.1/). Valid examples live in [`../../examples/v0.1/`](../../examples/v0.1/). The benchmark catalog is [`../../benchmarks/catalog/v0.1.json`](../../benchmarks/catalog/v0.1.json), and the initial conformance profile is [`../../gauntlet/v0.1/default.profile.json`](../../gauntlet/v0.1/default.profile.json).

## Evidence hierarchy

Evidence records separate source kind, claim classification and strength. Normative standards are requirements within their applicability. Controlled benchmark or peer-reviewed evidence may be strong. Product documentation proves documented capability, not user outcome. Community signals are valuable discovery evidence but usually weak prevalence/causality evidence. Vendor claims remain vendor claims until independently verified.

Strength is never inferred solely from popularity. Conflicts and limitations remain first-class.

## Decisions and contracts

Design Decision Records preserve why: problem, evidence, alternatives, criteria, decision, trade-offs, risks, exceptions, implementation, validation and review triggers.

Versioned Design Contracts define what is valid. Their lifecycle is experimental → candidate → verified → canonical → deprecated → retired. Every contract separates **hard constraints**, **creative territory**, and **unresolved questions** so agents do not confuse requirements with opportunities or silently invent missing intent.

## Tokens

[`../../tokens/v0.1/byjtt.tokens.json`](../../tokens/v0.1/byjtt.tokens.json) is the minimal DTCG 2025.10 baseline. It demonstrates typed groups, color objects, dimensions, durations and aliases. It is deliberately small and provisional; values must earn canonical status through real product work.

## Design Context Package and compiler contract

The Design Context Package (DCP) is a generic, model-agnostic compiler output. A future compiler reads pinned evidence, decisions, contracts, patterns and benchmark targets, resolves references, preserves unknowns, separates constraint classes, and emits a deterministic package plus source digest.

Adapters may serialize a DCP for GPT-5.6 Sol Light, CommandCode, free/open/local models, Claude `/design`, Figma, Stitch, v0, Lovable, Replit or Superdesign. No adapter owns canonical intent, and no paid Claude or Gemini subscription is required. Initial benchmark work should prioritize GPT-5.6 Sol Light, CommandCode, and credible free/open/local models.

## Future local/free assistant

The future assistant is retrieval over evidence, decisions, contracts, patterns and benchmarks. Its core responses should support **explain**, **show evidence**, **compile context**, **validate**, and **teach**. v0.1 does not embed a heavyweight local-model runtime. Retrieval indexes and inference backends should remain replaceable; deterministic schema/reference validation stays outside the model.

## Gauntlet/conformance

The Gauntlet separates static, runtime and human/experiential conformance. A passing automated scan is never presented as full accessibility or design proof. Required checks retain evidence, evaluator kind and release policy. Human review remains explicit where judgment is irreducible.

## What remains

- validate the schemas against real projects and version them from evidence;
- implement deterministic DCP compilation and reference resolution;
- add adapter fixtures without making providers dependencies;
- map the existing SaaS benchmark to the complete catalog;
- calibrate Gauntlet rubrics and known false positives/negatives;
- publish useful public surfaces only after the underlying records are credible;
- evaluate retrieval architecture before selecting any local inference runtime.
